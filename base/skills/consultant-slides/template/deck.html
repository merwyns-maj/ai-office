Northwind Foods · Margin programme

A path to a 12% EBITDA margin by 2028

Steering committee pre-read: diagnosis, value levers and a phased plan for decision
26 November 2026  ·  Strategy & Transformation team
Executive summary
Draft for discussion

Five levers can lift EBITDA margin from 7.9% to 12.0% by 2028 if the steering committee approves phase 1 this month

Situation

Northwind has grown revenue 13% since 2023 and holds the number two share in chilled foods. The board target is a 12% EBITDA margin by 2028.

Complication

Margin has fallen 1.6 pts in three years. Logistics cost grew twice as fast as revenue, and procurement is spread across 340 suppliers with no category ownership.

Resolution
  1. Procurement and logistics close two thirds of the gap. Category management and network redesign add 2.5 pts with proven playbooks.
  2. Pricing, SKU and overhead levers add the last 1.6 pts and need less capital, but depend on commercial buy-in.
  3. A phased plan starts with quick wins in Q1 2027 and funds phase 2 from phase 1 savings. Peak investment is IDR 85 bn.
  4. Decision required today: approve phase 1 scope, budget and a full-time programme lead.
Note: margins are EBITDA as % of net revenue, FY basis. Source: Northwind management accounts FY2023 to FY2026 (illustrative); team analysis

Logistics cost grew twice as fast as revenue since 2023

Index, FY2023 = 100
Net revenueLogistics cost

So what

Logistics cost rose 27% against 13% revenue growth, taking 1.1 pts off margin on its own.

  • Network: 9 depots serve a footprint that 6 could cover
  • Drop size: average drop fell 18% as small-format stores grew
  • Contracts: 70% of freight is on spot rates
Note: logistics cost includes warehousing, primary and secondary freight. Source: Northwind management accounts FY2023 to FY2026 (illustrative); team analysis

Methodology and definitions

Definitions

EBITDA margin
EBITDA as % of net revenue, before one-off programme costs
Run-rate
Annualised impact once an initiative is fully delivered
Logistics cost
Warehousing, primary and secondary freight, excluding plant handling
Tail SKU
SKUs in the bottom 20% of contribution, together under 3% of revenue

Method and data sources

Spend analysis
FY2026 supplier spend cube, 340 suppliers, mapped to 22 categories
Benchmarks
Peer ranges from published annual reports; outliers removed
Impact sizing
Bottom-up by initiative, validated in six workshops
Financial model
Team model v3; nominal IDR, no inflation uplift
