## What this means for the skill
- The method (answer first, pyramid, action titles, sourced charts, takeaway box) is consistent across every source and goes into the skill as rules.
- The firms' brand specs (colours, typefaces) were not confirmed from primary sources, and they belong to the firms anyway. The skill uses its own neutral tokens and forbids copying a firm's brand (C2).
- Chart, grid, type and colour values are office rules: starting values judged on the rendered template, not sourced numbers.
